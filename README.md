# Caowl Studios

I’m Adam Khalil, frontend developer and co-owner at Caowl Studios in Stockholm. Our studio website gives prospective clients a way to explore our work, see how we approach a project, and get in touch.

[Visit the live website](https://www.caowlstudios.se/) · [Watch the studio showreel](https://www.caowlstudios.se/#showreel)

## A closer look at the interface

- **Studio philosophy.** Four expandable cards let visitors skim the principles, then open the one they want to read.
- **Services.** The service selector groups our work into marketing, branding, development, and hosting. Choose *Utveckling* to explore development.
- **Contact.** Visitors can switch between sending a project brief and booking a conversation within the same section.
